You can request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. Career Solutions for Today publishes more than one app, and each has its own data. Pick the app you want to delete data from:

Program Shortcuts — Delete Account & Data

Android app · com.careersolutionsfortoday.systemshortcuts

What Program Shortcuts stores about you

  • Firebase Authentication account — email address, provider link (Google or email/password), display name, and Google profile photo when supplied.
  • Firestore record at shortcutsUsage/{uid} — trial start date, most recent visit timestamp, purchase flag, and the version of the Terms and Privacy Policy you accepted with its timestamp.
  • Local device data — favorites, interface preferences, and cached policy-acceptance flags stored on your device only. See the Program Shortcuts Privacy Policy for the full breakdown.
  • Google Play Billing — purchase history is held by Google Play, not by us. See Refunds and purchases below.

How to request deletion

Program Shortcuts does not currently offer an in-app deletion button. Additional in-app deletion controls may be provided in a future release. Until then, request deletion by email:

  • To: stevenk@careersolutionsfortoday.com
  • Subject: Delete My Program Shortcuts Data
  • In the body, tell us which one of these you want:
    1. Program Shortcuts data only — remove the Firestore record at shortcutsUsage/{uid}. Your Firebase account stays active so you can continue using Resume Builder and any other Career Solutions services on the same email.
    2. Full account deletion — remove the Firestore record AND delete the Firebase Authentication account. This ends access to all Career Solutions apps on that email address, including Resume Builder.

Please send the request from the email address on the account so we can verify ownership. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days of a valid request; most requests are processed within 72 hours.

Local device data (favorites, preferences)

Your favorites and interface preferences are stored only on your device. They are removed automatically when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Server-side deletion does not touch this on-device information.

Refunds and purchases

Google Play holds your purchase history. Requesting account deletion does not automatically refund a Google Play purchase. Refund eligibility is governed by Google Play’s refund policies:

  • Within 48 hours of purchase, request the refund directly through the Google Play Store.
  • Between 48 hours and 7 days after purchase, email support with your Google Play order ID and we will submit a refund request on your behalf. Approval remains subject to Google’s policies.

What may be retained (and why)

After deletion, records may be retained by us or our service providers only when necessary for the following reasons and for the minimum period required by law:

  • Legal, tax, or financial-recordkeeping obligations.
  • Fraud investigations or refund/dispute handling relating to your purchase.
  • Encrypted operational backups maintained by our service providers, which are overwritten on their normal backup rotation.

The Program Shortcuts Privacy Policy describes retention in more detail.

Option 1 — Delete From Inside the App (Fastest)

If you can still sign in, you can delete your account and data yourself in under a minute:

  1. Open the Career Resume Builder app (or visit careersolutionsfortoday.com/resumebuilder.html) and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile avatar in the top-right corner to open the account menu.
  3. Select "Delete Account & Data".
  4. Confirm the deletion in the pop-up. Your account, resumes, career plans, uploaded photos, and saved settings are removed immediately.
Note: If you have an active paid subscription, you must cancel it first. This prevents future charges after deletion.
  • Google Play subscription (Android app): open the Google Play Store app → tap your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → tap Career Resume Builder → Cancel subscription. Or visit play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions in a browser.
  • Web (Stripe) subscription: in the app or on the website, open the user menu and tap Manage Subscription. You will be sent to Stripe’s billing portal to cancel.

Cancellation stops future renewals. You will keep access for the remainder of the period you have already paid for.

Option 2 — Request Deletion by Email

If you no longer have access to the app, have uninstalled it, or prefer we handle it for you, send us an email:

  • To: stevenk@careersolutionsfortoday.com
  • Subject: Delete My Account and Data
  • Include in the body:
    • The email address associated with your account
    • A brief confirmation: "I request permanent deletion of my account and data."

Please send the request from the email address on the account so we can verify ownership. We will confirm the deletion and complete it within 30 days of receiving a valid request (most requests are processed within 72 hours).

What Gets Deleted

When your request is processed, the following is permanently removed from our active systems:

  • Your Firebase Authentication record (email, Google sign-in link, password hash)
  • Your profile (name, contact info, preferences)
  • All saved resumes, 90-day plans, and job-application tracking data
  • Uploaded files, including profile photos and parsed resume documents
  • Usage records (free-tier counters, AI-usage counters, analysis cache)
  • Any published share links associated with your resumes
